YT Rank and Tag Checker

Apni video kisi keyword par kis position par aa rahi hai, ek saath kai keywords me check karo. Kisi bhi video ke tags bhi nikal sakte ho.

RankKeywordIs keyword par #1 video

Ye result YouTube Data API ke search results par based hain. Asli YouTube app ki feed personalization ki wajah se thodi alag ho sakti hai, isliye isse ek mote andaze ki tarah use karo. Har keyword check me 100 quota units lagte hain (Top 100 me 200). Quota counter sirf is browser ka andaza hai, asli quota Google ke dashboard me dikhta hai. Quota har raat Pacific time ke midnight par reset hota hai (India me lagbhag dopahar 12:30 se 1:30 baje). Ek din ka result is browser me save rehta hai, to wahi keyword dobara check karne par quota nahi lagta.
